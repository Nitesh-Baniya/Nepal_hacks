**Risk Management Policy**

Approved by [Signature] on **03/11/2025**, this policy outlines DanfeCorp's structured approach to identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks that may impact its operations, assets, data, and reputation.

**Purpose and Scope**
The policy establishes a comprehensive risk management framework to ensure risk management practices are integrated into all business processes and decision-making activities. It applies to all DanfeCorp employees, contractors, business units, and third-party vendors involved in handling organizational assets, data, and operations.

**Roles and Responsibilities**

* Risk Management Team: Oversees the risk management program and ensures compliance.
* Executive Leadership: Provides strategic direction and allocates resources for risk management initiatives.
* Business Unit Managers: Identify and manage risks within their respective areas.
* Security Team: Conducts risk assessments related to cybersecurity threats.
* Employees: Report potential risks and adhere to established risk mitigation guidelines.

**Risk Management Framework**

DanfeCorp follows a comprehensive risk management framework, which includes:

1. **Risk Identification**: Utilize risk registers, security assessments, incident reports, and industry threat intelligence to identify potential risks.
2. **Risk Assessment**: Assess risks based on Likelihood (Low, Medium, High) and Impact (Low, Medium, High) using qualitative and quantitative methodologies.
3. **Risk Mitigation and Treatment**:
	* Risk Avoidance: Eliminate activities that introduce unacceptable risks.
	* Risk Reduction: Implement controls to minimize risks.
	* Risk Transfer: Shift risks through insurance, outsourcing, or contractual agreements.
	* Risk Acceptance: Accept risks within DanfeCorp's risk tolerance.
4. **Risk Monitoring and Reporting**: Continuously monitor risks, track remediation efforts, conduct periodic risk reviews, and report significant risks to executive leadership.

**Incident and Crisis Management**
DanfeCorp maintains comprehensive incident and crisis management procedures to address high-impact risks when they materialize.

**Compliance and Regulatory Considerations**
Ensure alignment with applicable regulatory requirements, industry standards, and best practices through regular audits and assessments.

**Policy Review and Updates**
The policy shall be reviewed and updated annually or in response to significant changes in DanfeCorp's business operations, regulatory landscape, or risk environment.
