**DanfeCorp Password Management Policy**

**Purpose:** Establishes standards for password creation, management, and protection to ensure security of DanfeCorp's systems, applications, and data.

**Scope:** Applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and individuals accessing DanfeCorp's systems, networks, or data.

**Password Requirements:**

1. **Password Complexity:**
	* Minimum length: 12 characters
	* Must contain at least one uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character
	* No commonly used words, personal information, or company-specific terms
2. **Password Expiration and Rotation:**
	* User passwords: change every 90 days
	* System and privileged account passwords: change every 60 days
	* Password history: users may not reuse the last five passwords
3. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):**
	* Mandatory for accessing sensitive or confidential data
	* Required for all remote access and privileged accounts

**Privileged Account Management:**

1. **Unique Credentials:** Privileged accounts use unique, complex passwords differing from regular user accounts
2. **Dedicated Credentials:** Privileged accounts must have dedicated credentials not shared with others
3. **Rotation and Storage:** Privileged passwords rotated at least every 60 days and stored in a secure password vault

**Account Lockout Policy:**

* Accounts will be locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts

**Policy Compliance and Enforcement:**

1. **Audits:** Regular password audits to ensure compliance
2. **Disciplinary Actions:** Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action, up to termination
3. **Review Cycle:** The IT Security Team reviews and updates the policy at least annually

**Implementation:**

* All departments must align operations with this policy within 30 days of effective date
* Passwords must be stored using industry-standard hashing algorithms and never transmitted unencrypted

For questions or clarifications, contact the Security Team via secure channels only.
