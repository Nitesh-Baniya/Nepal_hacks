**DanfeCorp Data Center Security Policy**

This policy outlines protocols and controls to ensure the security, integrity, and availability of DanfeCorp's data and IT infrastructure hosted at its co-location data centers. The policy applies to all assets housed at the data centers, including servers, networking equipment, storage devices, and personnel who access these assets.

**Physical Security Controls**

DanfeCorp implements robust physical security measures, including:

1. Site selection and design:
	* Primary location: Dhulikhel, Nepal
	* Alternate site (Availability Zone): Duhabi, Nepal
	* Redundancy: N+1 configurations to ensure continuous operation
2. Perimeter security:
	* Fencing and barriers to prevent unauthorized entry
	* Access points with security controls to monitor and restrict access
3. Physical access controls:
	* Multi-layered authentication mechanisms (biometric scans, access cards)
	* Access authorization: only personnel with legitimate business need and proper authorization
	* Visitor management: pre-approved visitors, registered upon arrival, and escorted at all times
4. Surveillance and monitoring:
	* Continuous video surveillance covering critical areas (entrances, exits, server rooms)
	* Intrusion detection systems to alert security personnel of unauthorized access attempts
5. Environmental controls:
	* Asset management: inventory and cataloging of equipment, with accurate asset records
	* Secure disposal of decommissioned equipment

**Network Security Controls**

DanfeCorp employs stringent network security controls, including:

1. Network segmentation: limiting access between systems based on role and function
2. Firewalls and intrusion detection systems (IDS): monitoring and controlling network traffic
3. Regular audits to ensure compliance with security policies and identify potential vulnerabilities

**Compliance and Standards**

DanfeCorp ensures that operations at both data centers comply with all relevant regulatory requirements and adhere to recognized industry security standards, including:

1. Regulatory Compliance: local and international regulations, ISO 27001 for information security management
2. Regular Assessments: security assessments and audits to ensure ongoing compliance

**Incident Response**

DanfeCorp maintains a comprehensive incident response program to handle any data center security breaches or emergencies swiftly and effectively.

**Review and Maintenance**

This policy is reviewed at least annually, or whenever significant changes occur to the company's systems, business requirements, or external regulations. Feedback from security audits, assessments, and incident responses is used to continuously improve data center security measures.

This policy document supersedes all previous data center security guidelines and becomes effective as of the specified date. All departments must align their operations with these requirements within 30 days of the effective date.
