**Logging and Monitoring Policy**

**Purpose**: This policy defines the logging and monitoring requirements necessary to detect, investigate, and respond to security incidents and operational issues. It ensures that DanfeCorp maintains an effective security posture by capturing and analyzing relevant system and network activity across the enterprise infrastructure.

**Scope**: This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and individuals who access DanfeCorp's systems, applications, networks, or data. It covers all IT assets, including servers, workstations, applications, databases, and network devices, regardless of physical location or hosting environment.

**Logging Requirements**

1. **Log Sources**: Comprehensive logs must be enabled for critical components, including:
	* Authentication and Access Control Systems
	* Operating Systems and Servers
	* Network Devices
	* Databases and Critical Applications
	* Security Tools
2. **Log Content**: Logs must capture relevant security and operational details, including:
	* System-level events
	* Service starts/stops
	* Administrative actions
	* Firewalls, routers, and switches traffic patterns
	* Connection attempts
	* Configuration modifications
	* Transaction logging and audit trails
3. **Log Retention and Protection**: Log retention requirements balance operational needs, security investigations, and compliance obligations:
	* Timestamps
	* User and System Activities
	* Authentication Attempts
	* Privileged Access
	* Recorded with contextual information and emphasis on interactions with sensitive or regulated information

**Monitoring and Alerting**

1. **Continuous Monitoring**: Real-time monitoring and automated alerts must be implemented for security logs, with analyst review and periodic assessment to ensure detection capabilities remain aligned with the current threat landscape.
2. **Incident Detection and Response**: A process flow applies when security events are detected:
	* Retention Period: Minimum of one year
	* Storage: Secure, centralized management system
	* Integrity Protection: Strict access controls, cryptographic verification when possible

**Access Controls for Logs**

1. Restricted Access: Only authorized personnel may access system logs based on job responsibilities and security clearance.
2. Role-Based Access: Controls must limit log visibility based on job roles and business necessity, with differentiated access levels separating standard operational access from security investigation privileges.
3. Regular Reviews: Access permissions must be conducted quarterly to prevent privilege creep.

**Policy Compliance and Enforcement**

1. Audits: Regular audits will be performed through automated assessment tools and manual review processes to verify compliance.
2. Disciplinary Action: Non-compliance may result in disciplinary actions, with the severity proportional to the compliance violation's potential impact.

This policy document supersedes all previous logging and monitoring guidelines and becomes effective as of the specified date. All departments must align their operations with these requirements within 30 days of the effective date.
