**DanfeCorp Information Security Policy**

Approved by [Signature] on 03/11/2025.

**Purpose**: Establishes the framework for safeguarding DanfeCorp's information assets, protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive data, and ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

**Scope**: Applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and third-party service providers accessing, managing, or processing DanfeCorp's data or systems.

**Objectives**:

1. Protect sensitive data and intellectual property.
2. Ensure business continuity and operational efficiency by guaranteeing availability of information systems and data.
3. Establish rigorous compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards for data protection and information security.
4. Identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with information security through regular evaluations and implementation of controls commensurate with risk levels.
5. Foster a culture of security awareness and responsibility across all levels of the organization.

**Information Security Governance**:

1. **ISMS**: Implement an Information Security Management System (ISMS) based on industry best practices and standards, incorporating development and enforcement of security policies, procedures, and controls.
2. **Information Security Committee**: Establish a dedicated committee to oversee implementation and ongoing management of the information security program.

**Classification and Handling**:

1. Classify data into public, internal, confidential, and highly confidential categories based on sensitivity and regulatory requirements.
2. Implement user access management principles of least privilege and need-to-know, with regular reviews to ensure appropriate access rights.
3. Use robust authentication mechanisms (e.g., multi-factor authentication) to protect sensitive data.

**Incident Management**:

1. Detect and report all security incidents immediately to the Information Security Team.
2. Maintain a comprehensive incident response plan to detect, contain, eradicate, and recover from incidents in a timely manner.
3. Conduct post-incident reviews to evaluate cause, impact, and response effectiveness, and use lessons learned to enhance future security measures.

**Compliance and Legal Requirements**:

1. Comply with applicable data protection and privacy regulatory laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) and industry standards through ongoing monitoring and policy updates.
2. Ensure third-party vendors and service providers adhere to DanfeCorp's security standards, enforced through contractual agreements and regular assessments.
3. Conduct regular audits and continuous monitoring of information systems to ensure compliance with this policy and detect any security gaps.

**Training and Awareness**:

1. Provide regular information security training to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors on their roles and responsibilities in protecting information.
2. Conduct periodic training sessions and awareness programs to keep personnel updated on emerging threats, new security tools, and changes in policies or procedures.

**Policy Review and Maintenance**:

1. Review this policy annually or after a major incident to ensure its continued effectiveness and alignment with changing business needs, regulatory requirements, and emerging security threats.
2. Use feedback from audits, incident reviews, and training initiatives to continuously enhance security policies, controls, and procedures.

This policy document supersedes all previous information security guidelines and becomes effective as of the date specified above.
