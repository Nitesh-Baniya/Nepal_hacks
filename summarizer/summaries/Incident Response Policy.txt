This Incident Response Policy outlines DanfeCorp's structured approach to responding to security incidents, aiming to minimize impact, preserve evidence, and ensure rapid recovery of information systems.

**Key Objectives:**

1. Rapidly identify, contain, and mitigate the effects of security incidents.
2. Preserve evidence for analysis and remediation.
3. Ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and systems.
4. Communicate effectively with stakeholders during the response phase.
5. Conduct regular reviews to update security measures and policies.

**Incident Response Process:**

1. Reporting: Employees, contractors, and third parties must report suspected security incidents immediately to the Incident Response Team (IRT).
2. Classification: Incidents will be classified into low, medium, or high severity based on their impact and risk.
3. Initial Response:
	* Disconnecting affected systems and disabling access.
	* Blocking malicious IP addresses and changing passwords.
	* Applying temporary fixes and controls.
4. Containment:
	* Disabling network segments and isolating affected areas.
	* Monitoring for signs of recurrence or abnormal behavior.
5. Recovery: Restoring systems from backups, reinstalling software, and monitoring post-recovery.
6. Post-Incident Review: Conducting a review to assess response effectiveness, identify process gaps, and document lessons learned.

**Incident Response Team (IRT):**

1. Lead the overall response and ensure adherence to the Incident Response Policy.
2. Handle technical investigation, containment, and eradication.
3. Provide guidance on legal obligations and ensures regulatory compliance.
4. Manage internal and external communications, including notifications to affected parties and regulators.
5. Offer strategic direction and decision-making for significant incidents.

**Compliance and Reporting:**

1. Regulatory Compliance: Adhering to applicable regulatory and industry-specific requirements.
2. Reporting Obligations: Documenting all incidents in accordance with internal policies and external regulatory requirements.

**Review and Testing:**

1. Regular incident response drills to test the effectiveness of the process.
2. Reviewing and updating this policy annually or following a major incident to ensure it remains effective and relevant.

**Documentation:**

1. Maintaining an incident log for all incidents, documenting key actions, decisions, and communications.
2. Preparing a detailed post-incident report for each incident, summarizing the event, response efforts, lessons learned, and recommendations for future improvements.
